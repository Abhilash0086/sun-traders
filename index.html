Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything contractors and builders typically ask before ordering.

What sizes of Renacon AAC Blocks are available?+
Standard block dimensions are 600mm (L) × 200mm (H). Widths are available in 75mm, 100mm, 150mm, 200mm, and 250mm to suit different wall thickness requirements.
Which width should I use for my project?+
100mm is common for internal partition walls, 150–200mm for external or load-bearing walls, and 75mm for infill panels. Our team is happy to advise based on your structural drawings at no charge.
Do you offer delivery to the construction site?+
Yes — we arrange direct delivery to your site. Reach out with your location and required quantity and we'll confirm availability and delivery schedule.
What is the minimum order quantity?+
We supply both small and large orders. Contact us with your requirement and we'll provide the best pricing based on quantity.
Are Renacon blocks certified?+
Yes. Renacon AAC Blocks are manufactured and tested to IS 2185 Part 3 standards — the Indian Standard for precast concrete masonry units.
Can AAC blocks be used for load-bearing walls?+
Renacon AAC Blocks are primarily designed for non-load-bearing and infill wall applications in framed structures (RCC or steel). For load-bearing applications, please consult your structural engineer.
Do I need a special mortar or adhesive?+
Thin-bed AAC block mortar or Renacon's own block-joining mortar is recommended for best results. Standard cement mortar can be used but thin-bed mortar reduces joints and improves thermal performance.
